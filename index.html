Applet Library

A collection of interactive web applets for mathematics and science education, created by Todd Edwards of Miami University, primarily as tools for teaching and learning. They are designed for hands-on mathematical exploration in the classroom. Each applet is self-contained with its own README and can be used independently.

Teaching & Learning

MarginaliaA reading-and-writing studio: read and annotate five source documents, and think in the margin. TelefoneTelephone, except the machine is every player. nVokeFind out how someone actually sees a shape. Sort LabAn open-sort surface for talking about how learners categorize. Watch Your Own ThinkingThree metacognitive math puzzles that record how you solve, then hand the reasoning back to you. Standards CrosswordPick a framework, grade and standard, then solve a crossword that reviews it. CliqueA Connections-style word puzzle game for the classroom. PEMDAS WordleA Wordle-like game for equations. Orbit ExplorerExplore the dynamics of Collatz-style functions: watch the trajectory and any cycle it falls into.

Retro Educational Games

DragonkeepDraw a dungeon floor plan and walk through it. Volume, surface area, and a dragon you win by not killing. Haunt Under constructionYou are the ghosts. The computer plays the hero; you switch among four ghosts to corner him before he clears the maze. Chomp & Spell Under constructionEat the letters, then spell the words. A maze chase that ends in a Scrabble-scored spelling round. Circuit Breaker Under constructionLights out. Robots in the maze. Six bullets. Survive one endless night by the light you carry. Vanishing Point Under constructionFive beams, one horizon. Hop the beams, clear the saucers, torpedo the ship -- and flip to the side view to see how perspective draws it. Lantern Run Under constructionA zoologist in the Amazon, by lantern at night and binoculars by day. Find twenty-four species across the four layers of the rainforest.
