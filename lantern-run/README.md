# Lantern Run

An expedition in the Amazon. Twenty-four species, by night and by day. Thirty minutes. A retro jungle game for an NES-style controller.

## How to play

You're a zoologist. The rainforest has four layers: the **forest floor**, the **understory**, the **canopy** and the **emergent** treetops, and each species lives in its own. Twelve species come out **by night** and twelve **by day**. Find all twenty-four before your time runs out.

To document a species, **hold B** and **stand still** with the animal in view until the circle fills.

- **By night** you see only what your lantern reaches. Holding B raises it so you see further. Animals' eyes shine in the dark before you can see the rest of them.
- **By day** you can see everything, and so can they. Holding B brings up binoculars, so you can observe from much further away (face the animal). Run near a shy animal and it bolts: birds fly off, monkeys scatter, an iguana drops off its branch. Creep (hold B while moving) to get closer.

**Switching night and day:** open the field journal with **Select** and press **B**. Dusk or dawn falls over two seconds, and the night animals slip away as the day ones come out, or the other way round. Nocturnal species only appear at night, diurnal ones only by day. Jaguars, anacondas and caimans are about at both times.

| Button | Keyboard | Does |
|---|---|---|
| D-pad | Arrows / WASD | Run. Up or down beside a liana to climb it. |
| A | Z, K or Space | Jump. Hold it to jump higher. Down + A drops you through a branch. On the swinging vine, A or Down lets go. |
| B | X or J | Hold: lantern up (night) or binoculars (day), creep slowly, and observe. |
| Select | Shift | Field journal: night and day pages (Left/Right), a true fact about each species you've found. B turns night to day; A turns sound on or off. |
| Start | Enter (or P) | Start / pause. |

A standard USB gamepad works too, including cheap pads that report the D-pad as an analog stick.

**The jungle** is a loop: keep going one way and you come back to where you started. Creeks cross the forest floor. Some have a vine to swing across; caimans live in others, and you can hop across their heads while their jaws are shut. At night, lightning lights the whole forest now and then. Look while you can.

**Watch out for**: jaguars, anacondas and the fer-de-lance (it can sense your warmth in total darkness). A vampire bat bite costs you time, and so does touching a poison dart frog: never touch one. A startled armadillo jumps straight up.

Browsers start sound only after a key press or click (a gamepad button may not count), so press any key once if it is silent.

Points: 500 for a species on the forest floor, more the higher it lives. A time bonus if you find all twenty-four.
