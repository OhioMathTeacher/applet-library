# Lantern Run

A night in the Amazon. Twelve species. Twenty minutes. A retro jungle game for an NES-style controller.

## How to play

You're a zoologist with one lantern and one night. The rainforest has four layers: the **forest floor**, the **understory**, the **canopy** and the **emergent** treetops. Each species lives in its own. Find all twelve and document them before dawn.

To document a species, **hold B** to raise your lantern, get the animal in your light, and **stand still** until the circle fills. Some animals are shy: run near them and they leap away, or fly off and come back later.

| Button | Keyboard | Does |
|---|---|---|
| D-pad | Arrows / WASD | Run. Up or down beside a liana to climb it. |
| A | Z, K or Space | Jump. Hold it to jump higher. Down + A drops you through a branch. On the swinging vine, A or Down lets go. |
| B | X or J | Hold: raise the lantern (you see further), creep slowly, and observe. |
| Select | Shift | Field journal: what you've found, and a fact about each. A in the journal turns sound on or off. |
| Start | Enter (or P) | Start / pause. |

A standard USB gamepad works too, including cheap pads that report the D-pad as an analog stick.

**The jungle** is a loop: keep going one way and you come back to where you started. Creeks cross the forest floor. Some have a vine to swing across; caimans live in others, and you can hop across their heads while their jaws are shut. Lightning lights the whole forest now and then. Look while you can.

**Watch out for**: jaguars, anacondas and the fer-de-lance (it can sense your warmth in total darkness). Vampire bats bite (it costs you time), and a startled armadillo jumps straight up.

Browsers start sound only after a key press or click (a gamepad button may not count), so press any key once if it is silent.

Points: 500 for a species on the forest floor, more the higher it lives. A time bonus if you find all twelve.
