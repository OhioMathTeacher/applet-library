The 196 Problem

Reverse a number and add. Repeat until it reads the same both ways. Does 196 ever get there?

Try it

57 + 75 = 132132 + 231 = 363 ✓196 + 691 = 887…

What we know

Computers have followed 196 for hundreds of millions of digits. Still no palindrome.

Nobody has proved that any number never gets there.

Ask your students

Which two-digit numbers finish in one step? Why those?

89 and 98 take the same number of steps. Why?
