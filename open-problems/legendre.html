Legendre's Conjecture

Between any two perfect squares that sit next to each other, there’s always a prime.

Try it

Between 4 and 9: 5, 7Between 16 and 25: 17, 19, 23Between 100 and 121: five primes

What we know

In 1912 it made a famous list of four prime problems that seemed out of reach. All four are still unsolved.

We do know there’s always a prime between any number and its double.
