Lonely Runner Conjecture

Runners start together on a round track, all at different speeds. Will every runner, at some moment, be far from everyone else?

Try it

2 runners: far means half a lap3 runners: far means a third of a lap4 runners: a quarter

What we know

It’s proved for up to seven runners.

For eight or more, nobody knows.

Ask your students

Where is each runner after half a minute? A third of a minute?

Act it out: three students, three speeds. Freeze! Who’s lonely?
