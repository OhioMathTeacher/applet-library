Odd Perfect Numbers

A number is perfect when its factors add up to it, like 6 = 1 + 2 + 3. Is any perfect number odd?

Try it

6 = 1 + 2 + 328 = 1 + 2 + 4 + 7 + 14Next: 496, then 8,128

What we know

About fifty perfect numbers are known. Every one is even.

People have wondered about odd ones for more than 2,000 years.

Ask your students

Sort 1 to 30: factors add to less, more, or exactly?

Can an odd number’s factors ever add to more than it? (Try 945.)
