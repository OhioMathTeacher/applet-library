Twin Prime Conjecture

Primes that are just 2 apart, like 11 and 13, keep showing up forever.

Try it

3 and 517 and 1941 and 43

What we know

We’ve known for over 2,000 years that primes never run out.

Since 2013 we know primes keep coming within 246 of each other forever. Getting that down to 2 is the unsolved part.

Ask your students

Look at the number between each twin pair: 4, 6, 12, 18, 30… What do you notice?

Can three primes in a row be 2 apart, like 3, 5, 7? Ever again?
