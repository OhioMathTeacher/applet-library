# Tidewall

A city on a sea wall, at night. Raiders overhead, three cannons and a searchlight. A retro defence game for an NES-style controller.

## How to play

Six buildings and three cannon posts stand on a sea wall. Raiders fly across the sky in four **altitude bands**, left to right and right to left, and each time they turn round they come back one band lower. Once a raider reaches the lowest band it **dives and bombs** whatever it's aiming for. Shoot it down before then.

**The sky is dark.** You see a raider only where a searchlight beam touches it. You steer one beam yourself. Two more sweep on their own from the lamp towers, but only while those towers stand. Outside the beams, a raider shows only as a winking running light, and you can hear each one throbbing on its side of the screen, faster the lower it flies. You can shoot blind if you trust your ears.

**The tide rises.** Every building that falls lets the sea climb higher up the wall, flooding it from the low end. When all six buildings are gone, or all three cannons, the city falls. Every 10,000 points one fallen building is rebuilt and the sea draws back.

| Button | Keyboard | Does |
|---|---|---|
| B | X or J | Fire the **left** cannon (it shoots up and to the right). |
| A | Z, K or Space | Fire the **right** cannon (it shoots up and to the left). |
| D-pad Up | Up or W | Fire the **centre** cannon (straight up). |
| D-pad Left / Right | Arrows / A, D | Steer your searchlight. |
| Select | Shift | Flare: lights the whole sky for about three seconds. You start with three and get one more each wave (five at most). |
| Start | Enter (or P) | Start / pause. The pause screen shows the controls and points; Select there turns sound on or off. |

A standard USB gamepad works too, including cheap pads that report the D-pad as an analog stick. On the keyboard, M also turns sound on or off.

Browsers start sound only after a key press or click (a gamepad button may not count), so press any key once if it is silent.

**Raiders:** skiff 100, barge 250 (takes two hits), hawk 300 (fast), bandit 1000 (stays high and bombs from the top band, from wave 3). A bomb shot out of the air is 50. Each wave you hold earns a bonus for everything still standing. Waves get faster.
